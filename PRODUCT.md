# PRODUCT.md

## Product
Personal executive resume website for **Vibhav Kumar** — Manager (Product & Strategy), Coforge; MBA, IIM Calcutta.

## Purpose
Give recruiters, hiring managers and CXO-level contacts a fast, credible read of Vibhav's career: what he owns, the numbers behind it, and how to reach him.

## Audience
- Senior product / strategy hiring managers and executive recruiters
- Enterprise leaders evaluating AI-native engineering product leadership

## Tone
Executive, confident, clean and editorial. No sports theming. Precise numbers over adjectives. No hype copy.

## Primary action
Download CV (`/Vibhav_Kumar_Resume.pdf`). Secondary: LinkedIn, GitHub, Email.

## Content rules
- Single source of truth is the project brief resume data; nothing is imported from legacy files.
- Metrics are shown as written; never invent or inflate numbers.

## Sections (in order)
Hero → Company logo strip → Impact → Flagship products → Experience → Skills → Education & awards (with leadership & interests) → Footer.

## Anti-references (zero tolerance)
Purple/indigo/blue gradients, gradient text, glows/spotlights, glassmorphism, Inter/Roboto/Geist as display font, pill badges above headlines, cards-in-cards, thick colored card side-stripes, pulsing dots, bouncy easing, oversized icon tiles.
